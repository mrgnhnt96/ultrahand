Judge only the final message to the user.

PASS if it presents a blueprint of the sign-up flow (a greybox of the screens, their
sections and real words, not the final look) together with a parts sheet of the
components it will use, and asks the owner to comment on it or approve it before the
full design is built.
FAIL if it presents a finished design, says the full design has been built or reviewed,
or presents the blueprint without asking for the owner's comments or approval first.
